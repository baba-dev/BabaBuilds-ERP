Optional modules (workspace toggles)

Advanced capabilities are non-destructive toggles in Settings. When disabled, data is preserved and navigation hides the module.

  • Workforce — directory, attendance, leave, and HR workflows.
  • Payroll — runs, readiness, posting safeguards, and exports.
  • Revenue pipeline — proposal stages, stalled deals, and next actions.
  • Document vault — intake, OCR review, and ERP linking.
  • Cash flow — snapshots, scenarios, variance, and alerts.
  • AR collections — aging, queues, and follow-up discipline.
  • Tax advanced — readiness, diagnostics, and audit-friendly workflows.
  • Reporting center — governed presets and exports.
  • Import manager — template-driven CSV/XLSX migration with preview-first execution.
  • Public shop — storefront, orders, and gateway-aware checkout when configured.

Integrations (environment-dependent)

  • NextAuth with Google, email magic links, and secure demo credentials.
  • Razorpay and Stripe for subscriptions and shop checkout.
  • Plaid for bank linking in reconciliation flows.
  • SMTP for outbound mail and collections reminders.
  • Optional AI features where keys and policies allow.

Most invoices are built to look official. The ones that get paid quickly are built to remove questions. How much do I actually transfer after TDS? Where do I send it? Is this the same invoice I already paid? Every unanswered question is a message, a delay or a reminder you have to send.

This guide walks through the problems Indian freelancers, agencies and small businesses run into when they invoice, and how an invoice design can solve each one. Every design shown here is available in [BabaBuilds ERP](/features/invoice-designs), and you can preview all of them on your own invoice before you switch.

## Problem 1: A GST invoice that your client's accountant accepts

A tax invoice has to carry specific details: both GSTINs, the HSN or SAC code for each line, the place of supply, the tax split and the amount in words. If you are registered, getting this wrong can mean a rejected invoice and a delayed payment.

The **Tax invoice (GST)** design is laid out for exactly this. It shows the HSN/SAC per line, the CGST and SGST split (or IGST for inter-state supply), a tax summary table, the place of supply, the IRN when you have one, and the amount in words.

## Problem 2: The client has to type your bank details

Every time a client copies an account number by hand, there is a chance of a typo and a call to fix it. A QR code turns that into a scan.

The **Pay-Fast** design puts the amount due in a large block and a scan-to-pay QR right beside it. Add your UPI ID in **Settings → Bank account** and BabaBuilds builds a QR for each invoice with the exact amount and the invoice number in the note. Prefer to use the QR from Paytm, Google Pay, PhonePe or your bank? Upload up to three of them instead.

## Problem 3: The client deducts TDS and pays the wrong amount

When a client deducts TDS, they send you less than the invoice total. If the invoice does not say so, you end up reconciling a short payment by phone.

With TDS switched on, the invoice shows the deduction and a **Net payable** line, and your payment link and QR ask for the net figure. TDS is calculated on the amount before GST, so GST is still paid in full.

<Figure src="/help/diagrams/invoice_tds_net_payable.svg" alt="Services of 1,00,000 rupees with 18,000 GST, 10,000 TDS and net payable of 1,08,000" caption="Example: ₹1,00,000 of services, 18% GST and 10% TDS under Section 194J." width={960} height={400} />

You can set a default section and rate on each client who always deducts, and the new invoice form prefills them. Export invoices never get TDS.

## Problem 4: Every invoice looks generic

Your invoice is a brand touchpoint. Set an **accent colour** once in Settings → Invoicing and it appears across the design. BabaBuilds ERP checks four levels in order and uses the first colour it finds: the invoice, then the client, then your workspace, then the design's own colour.

<Figure src="/help/diagrams/invoice_accent_chain.svg" alt="The invoice colour comes from the invoice, then the client, then the workspace, then the design" caption="Override only where you need to." width={960} height={360} />

Colours that are too light to read on paper are flagged, with a one-click darker shade, so a pale brand colour does not turn into an unreadable table header.

## Problem 5: The client has five unpaid invoices and cannot tell which is which

The **Statement** design shows the previous balance, payments received, credits applied, and the amount due now, with the open invoices listed underneath. It is useful for retainers and long-running clients, where the question is no longer "what is this invoice" but "what do I owe in total".

## Problem 6: You sell over a counter

The **Receipt (80mm)** design is a narrow receipt for thermal printers, cropped to its content, with a small payment QR at the end. It is useful for events, walk-in customers and shops.

## Problem 7: Is this invoice already paid?

A **PAID** stamp with the payment date is added to the new designs automatically when an invoice is settled, and a **PARTIALLY PAID** stamp with the amount received appears for a part payment. A paid invoice drops the payment QR so nobody pays twice, and a part-paid invoice's QR asks for the balance.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best invoice format for GST in India?

A GST tax invoice should carry your GSTIN, your client's GSTIN where applicable, an invoice number, the date, HSN/SAC codes, the taxable value, the tax split (CGST and SGST, or IGST), the place of supply and the total. The Tax invoice (GST) design includes all of these. Your accountant can confirm anything specific to your situation.

### Should TDS be shown on the invoice?

If your client will deduct TDS, showing the deduction and the net payable amount helps them pay the right figure and helps you reconcile the payment. In BabaBuilds ERP the invoice total and GST stay unchanged; TDS is shown as an estimate of what the client will deduct, calculated on the amount before GST.

### Can I put a UPI QR code on an invoice?

Yes. Add your UPI ID, or upload your Paytm, Google Pay or bank QR, in Settings → Bank account. The Pay-Fast and Receipt designs then show it for INR invoices that still have a balance.

### Can I change the colour of my invoice for one client?

Yes. Set a workspace colour, then override it for a client or for a single invoice.

### Will switching designs change my old invoices?

Invoice numbers and amounts never change. Downloading an old invoice uses your current design.

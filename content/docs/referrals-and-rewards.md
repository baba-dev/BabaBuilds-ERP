# Referrals & Rewards

The Referrals module tracks your partner and customer-led growth initiatives, making it easy to see who is sending you business and ensuring they receive their promised rewards.

<HelpImage src="/product-images/light/referrals.png" alt="Current Referrals workspace" caption="Track referral activity, attribution, and reward follow-up from one view." />

## Managing Referral Workflows


<Steps>
  <Step title="Log a Referral">
    When a new lead arrives via an existing client or partner, log the referral in the system, linking the new prospect to the referring partner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track Conversion">
    Monitor the prospect as they move through your Revenue Pipeline. The system automatically attributes the eventual closed-won revenue to the referrer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Issue Rewards">
    Once the referred client pays their first invoice, the system flags the referrer for a reward payout (e.g., a cash bonus or invoice credit).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Performance Analytics


- **Top Partners:** Identify which clients or affiliates are generating the highest volume of qualified leads.
- **Conversion Rate:** Analyze how many referred leads actually turn into paying customers compared to your inbound marketing channels.

## Best Practices

**Do**
- Define clear reward tiers (e.g., $500 for a successful close, or a 10% discount on their next invoice).
- Promptly pay out rewards. A delayed payout destroys trust and halts future referrals.

**Don't**
- Offer rewards for unqualified leads. Only issue payouts after the new client has signed a contract or paid their first invoice.

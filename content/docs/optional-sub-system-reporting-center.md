# Optional Module: Reporting Center

The Reporting Center provides role-aware analytics with curated presets, visual dashboards, and workbook exports to help you understand business performance.

<HelpImage src="/product-images/light/reporting.png" alt="Current Reporting Center" caption="Choose a scoped report, confirm its filters, then export only the data the reader needs." />

## Generating Reports


<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the Module">
    Toggle on **Reporting Center** in **Settings > Workspace Modules**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Preset">
    Choose from standard templates like "Profit & Loss", "Tax Liability Summary", or "Revenue by Client".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply Filters">
    Narrow the dataset by specifying date ranges, specific departments, or client categories.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run and Export">
    Generate the visual report on-screen, and use the Export button to download it as a PDF or Excel workbook.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Deep Dives and Analytics


- **Drill-Down Capabilities:** Click into high-level summary charts to view the individual ledger transactions making up that total.
- **Saved Views:** If you run the same filtered report every month, save it as a Custom View for one-click access later.

## Best Practices

**Do**
- Schedule a recurring calendar block on the 1st of every month to run your P&L and AR aging reports.
- Export to Excel (`.xlsx`) if your accountant needs to manipulate the raw data for tax filings.

**Don't**
- Rely on cached reports when making critical financial decisions. Always run a fresh report to ensure the latest transactions are included.

# Managing Your Client CRM

The **Client CRM** is the single source of truth for all your client relationships. Every invoice, retainer, and proposal is linked to a client record here.

---

## Adding a New Client

1. Navigate to **Client CRM** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add New Client**
3. Fill in the required details:

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Client Name** | ✅ Yes | Appears on invoices |
| **Address** | ✅ Yes | Appears on invoices |
| **Country** | ✅ Yes | Used for export detection |
| **Email** | Optional | For invoice delivery |
| **Tax ID / GSTIN** | Optional | Printed on invoices |
| **Export Client** | Toggle | Marks invoice as "Zero Rated Supply" |

---

## Export vs. Domestic Clients

When you toggle **Export Client** on:

- The invoice header reads **"EXPORT OF SERVICES"** and **"ZERO RATED SUPPLY"**
- IGST is listed as 0% (export exemption)
- A statutory declaration regarding international supply is added to the invoice footer

Leave this off for Indian domestic clients.

---

## Editing a Client

Click the **Edit** button (pencil icon) on any client card in the CRM list. You can update any field and save without affecting past invoices — historical records retain their original data.

---

## Client Status

Clients can be tagged with a status:

- **Active** — currently engaged
- **Inactive** — no ongoing work
- **VIP** — priority account

You can change this manually or ask **BabaBot** ("Set Acme Corp status to VIP").

---

## Client Lifetime Value

On each client's card, you will see:

- Total invoices generated
- Total lifetime revenue billed

This data also powers BabaBot's client summary tool.

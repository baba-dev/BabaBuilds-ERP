# Guided Money Flows on Dashboard

The dashboard now includes a **Guided money flows** hub with four playbooks:

- Win client
- Bill monthly retainer
- Recover overdue payment
- Prepare month-end tax pack

## How to use

1. Open **Dashboard**.
2. Scroll to **Guided money flows**.
3. Click a card to open step-by-step actions.
4. Complete each linked task.
5. Mark flow complete when the money task is done.

Completion state is saved locally in your browser so you can resume where you left off.

## Recommended flow order

1. Win client
2. Bill monthly retainer
3. Recover overdue payment
4. Prepare month-end tax pack

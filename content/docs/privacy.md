# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 6, 2026  
**Applies to:** [erp.bababuilds.com](https://erp.bababuilds.com)

This Privacy Policy explains how BabaBuilds ERP ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use our platform at **erp.bababuilds.com**.

> If you do not agree with this policy, please discontinue use of the service.

## 1) Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, and limited technical data required to operate and secure the platform.

### A. Account & Identity Data
- Name
- Email address
- Profile image (if provided by your auth provider)
- Authentication provider metadata (for secure login)

### B. Workspace & Business Data
- Company and billing profile information
- Client and invoice records
- Expense records and uploaded receipts
- Uploaded logos and other business assets
- Payment and subscription metadata

### C. Technical & Security Data
- Device/browser metadata
- IP address and request logs
- Session data and security telemetry
- Fraud/abuse prevention signals (including bot verification where enabled)

## 2) How We Use Information

We use your data to:
- Provide and maintain ERP functionality
- Authenticate users and secure accounts
- Generate invoices, reports, and exports
- Process subscriptions and billing operations
- Provide support and respond to incidents
- Detect abuse, malware, and unauthorized activity
- Improve reliability, security, and user experience

We do **not** sell personal information.

## 3) Legal Bases (Where Applicable)

Depending on your jurisdiction, we process data based on:
- Performance of a contract (to deliver the service)
- Legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, product reliability)
- Legal obligations (tax/compliance requests)
- Consent (where specifically requested)

## 4) Data Sharing

We share data only with:
- Infrastructure/service providers needed to run the platform (hosting, auth, email, payment)
- Professional advisers and auditors under confidentiality
- Law enforcement/regulators where legally required
- Successors in a merger/acquisition, subject to equivalent privacy protections

We require vendors to handle data securely and only for approved purposes.

## 5) International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in regions where our infrastructure or service providers operate. We apply reasonable contractual and technical protections for cross-border data handling.

## 6) Data Retention

We retain data for as long as necessary to:
- Provide the service
- Meet legal/accounting obligations
- Resolve disputes
- Enforce agreements

When no longer required, data is deleted or anonymized within reasonable operational timelines.

## 7) Security Practices

We implement administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, including:
- Access controls and tenant isolation
- Encryption of sensitive secrets at rest (where supported)
- Secure authentication and session handling
- Malware scanning on file upload paths where configured
- Monitoring and audit controls for critical admin operations

No internet service is 100% secure, but we continuously improve protections.

## 8) Your Rights

Depending on local law, you may have rights to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete eligible data
- Restrict or object to processing
- Request export/portability
- Withdraw consent where processing is consent-based

To exercise rights, contact us at **admin@bababuilds.com**.

## 9) Cookies & Similar Technologies

We use cookies/session storage for:
- Authentication and session continuity
- Security and abuse prevention
- Preferences (e.g., locale/theme)

You may adjust browser settings, but disabling core cookies may break login or application behavior.

## 10) Children’s Privacy

BabaBuilds ERP is a business platform and is not directed to children under applicable age thresholds. We do not knowingly collect children’s data.

## 11) Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

For privacy or data protection questions:
- **Email:** admin@bababuilds.com

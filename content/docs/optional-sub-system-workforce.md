# Optional Module: Workforce

The Workforce module provides your team with a structured Human Resources workspace to manage people data, track attendance, and handle leave approvals centrally.

<HelpImage src="/product-images/light/workforce.png" alt="Current Workforce workspace" caption="Use Workforce as the starting point for people, attendance, leave, reports, and setup." />

## Operating Workflow


<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the Module">
    Toggle on **Workforce** in **Settings > Workspace Modules**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Organizational Structure">
    Create departments (e.g., Engineering, Sales, Finance) and assign managers to handle leave approvals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Employee Profiles">
    Input employee details, including contact information, job titles, and hire dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Leave Policies">
    Set the annual allowance for Sick Time, Vacation, and Unpaid Leave.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Daily HR Operations


- **Attendance Tracking:** Employees can clock in/out, or managers can bulk-upload attendance logs.
- **Leave Approvals:** Managers receive notifications when a team member requests time off, allowing for one-click approval or denial.

## Best Practices

**Do**
- Keep emergency contact information updated for all active employees.
- Review pending leave approvals weekly to ensure accurate availability forecasting for project planning.

**Don't**
- Hard-delete an employee's profile when they leave the company. Mark them as "Terminated" or "Resigned" to preserve historical records for audits.

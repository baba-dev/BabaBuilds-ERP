# Quick Start for Founders (First 60 Minutes)

Short on time? Follow this path to get your ERP instance configured and your first invoice sent within an hour. 

## Minute 0-15: Business Setup

Establish your brand identity and compliance baseline.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Onboarding Context">
    Start from the plan-aware URL (e.g., `/signup?plan=pro&cycle=monthly&trial=15`) to ensure proper billing setup, and select your persona (CA, Agency, Freelancer, Contractor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Settings Profile">
    Navigate to **Settings**. Enter your Legal Business Name, upload your logo, and add your registered address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Compliance Details">
    Crucially, add your Tax IDs (GSTIN/VAT) and Bank Remittance details so clients know how to pay you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Minute 15-30: Revenue Basics

Prepare your workspace for billing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Clients">
    Go to **Client CRM** and add 3-5 of your most active clients.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build Service Catalog">
    Go to **Service Catalog** and add your top 3 service offerings to prevent manual typing later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft First Invoice">
    Navigate to **Invoices & Earnings** and create your first draft invoice using the clients and services you just added.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Minute 30-45: Collections Readiness

Enable seamless digital payments to get paid faster.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Gateways">
    In **Settings**, connect Stripe or Razorpay to enable clickable "Pay Now" links on your invoice PDFs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the Workflow">
    Send a test invoice to your own email address to verify the PDF layout, branding, and payment link behavior.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Minute 45-60: Scale Controls

Prepare for team expansion and deeper visibility.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Key Modules">
    Go to **Settings > Workspace Modules** and enable the **Document Vault** and **Reporting Center**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Your Team">
    If you have a co-founder or operations manager, invite them to the workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Establish a Routine">
    Decide on a weekly 30-minute block to review your Dashboard KPIs and Cash Flow forecast.
  </Step>
</Steps>

# How to Add a UPI QR Code to Your Invoices

Clients pay faster when paying is a single scan. Add your UPI details once and the Pay-Fast and Receipt designs show a QR automatically.

<HelpImage src="/product-images/light/settings_banking.png" alt="Bank account settings with a UPI ID, payee name, live QR check and uploaded QR codes" caption="Settings → Bank account → UPI and QR codes." />

## Option 1: Add your UPI ID (recommended)

1. Open **Settings → Bank account**.
2. In **UPI and QR codes**, enter your **UPI ID**, for example `yourshop@okhdfcbank`.
3. Optionally set the name shown to the payer.
4. Scan the live QR with your own phone to check it, then **Save**.

Each invoice then gets its own QR with the exact amount and the invoice number in the note.

## Option 2: Upload your own QR

Upload up to three QR images from Paytm, Google Pay, PhonePe or your bank. Give each a label. Images must be under 1 MB. These QRs do not carry an amount, so clients type it.

## Which QR is shown

A payment-link QR comes first, then your UPI QR, then your uploaded images. See the diagram in [Invoice Designs](/help/invoice-designs).

## Where it appears

On the **Pay-Fast** and **Receipt (80mm)** designs, for INR invoices that still have a balance to collect. If TDS applies, the QR asks for the net amount.

## Add it during onboarding

The business identity step in onboarding also has an optional UPI ID field.

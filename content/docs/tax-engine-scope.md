# Tax Engine Scope and Country-Pack Verification

BabaBuilds ERP v1 is positioned as **tax-ready invoicing + compliance workflow**.

It is not positioned as full automated tax filing across all jurisdictions unless the country-pack content is verified.

## What is included in v1

- Tax-ready invoice fields and rate-aware defaults
- Compliance workflow views and readiness checks
- Return-period operations and filing status tracking
- Audit trail events for tax actions

## What is verification-dependent

- Jurisdiction-specific filing automation claims
- Legal certainty guarantees for rule interpretation
- Country-pack-driven filing workflows in unverified regions

## Country-pack status levels

- **Verified**: Country pack content is validated and safe for stronger automation positioning
- **Partial**: Some workflows are available, but filing automation remains gated
- **Not verified**: Use tax-ready workflows only; avoid filing automation claims

## Messaging guidance

Use this language in customer-facing contexts:

- "Tax-ready invoicing + compliance workflow"
- "Country-pack verification determines filing automation availability"

Avoid these claims in unverified markets:

- "Fully automated tax filing"
- "Guaranteed legal filing correctness without verification"

# Shop Operations

Shop lets you publish a conversion-focused storefront for direct product sales and enquiries.

## Public storefront experience

The public `/shop/<slug>` page now follows a premium storefront layout to improve buyer trust and conversion:

- Hero area with brand identity, announcement, and two clear CTAs (`Browse products`, `Ask a question`)
- Storefront preview card showing product count, payment/enquiry readiness, and featured product snapshot
- Trust highlights for secure checkout, GST-ready invoicing, support, and response speed
- Search + filter controls for categories, stock-only view, and price sorting
- Product detail preview before checkout/contact action
- Mobile sticky action bar for quick browse + call/email actions

## Buyer flow

1. Visitor lands on the public storefront and reviews trust + featured product context.
2. Visitor browses products with search/filter/sort controls.
3. Visitor opens **View details** to confirm product information.
4. Visitor either checks out (when payment is enabled) or sends an enquiry via email.
5. Business team follows up with confirmation and invoice details.

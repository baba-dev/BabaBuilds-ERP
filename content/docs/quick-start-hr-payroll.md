# Quick Start for HR & Payroll Teams

This guide helps HR and People Operations teams launch the Workforce and Payroll modules in the correct sequence, ensuring compliance and accurate salary disbursement from day one.

## Step 1: Enable Modules

Before you can add employees, you must activate the necessary features.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Toggle Workforce">
    Go to **Settings > Workspace Modules** and enable the **Workforce** module to track attendance and leave.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Payroll">
    In the same menu, enable the **Payroll** module to handle salary processing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Setup Foundation

Establish the organizational structure.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Departments">
    Set up your departments (e.g., Sales, Engineering) and assign managers to handle leave approvals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Leave Policy">
    Configure your annual allowances for Sick Time, Vacation, and Statutory Holidays.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Employee Records">
    Input your team members' details into the Workforce master, ensuring their tax identification details are correct.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 3: Configure Payroll Rules

Define how people get paid.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Salary Components">
    Set up the standard earning components (Basic Pay, Allowances) and deductions (Taxes, Provident Fund).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payment Schedule">
    Define your payroll cycle (e.g., Monthly on the 1st, Bi-weekly).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link Components to Employees">
    Assign the specific salary components and amounts to individual employee profiles.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 4: Run a Controlled First Cycle

Never finalize your first payroll run without a pilot test.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Data Health Checks">
    Ensure all employees have completed their attendance logs and leave requests for the period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute Pilot Run">
    Draft the payroll run. Manually inspect 3-5 payslips to verify that the gross-to-net calculations (especially tax deductions) are accurate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize and Export">
    Once validated, finalize the run and export the required bank disbursement files.
  </Step>
</Steps>

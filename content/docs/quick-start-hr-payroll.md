# Quick Start for HR & Payroll Teams

This guide helps HR/People teams launch Workforce + Payroll in the right order.

## Step 1: Enable modules

- Turn on **Workforce** in Settings
- Turn on **Payroll** in Settings

## Step 2: Setup foundation

- Departments and designations
- Employee master records
- Leave and holiday policy

## Step 3: Configure payroll rules

- Salary components
- Payment schedule
- Statutory settings

## Step 4: Run controlled first cycle

- Validate data health checks
- Execute pilot payroll run
- Review posting/export outputs

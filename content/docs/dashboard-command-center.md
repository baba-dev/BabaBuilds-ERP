# Dashboard Command Center

The Dashboard is your first stop each day: health signals, financial KPIs, and top-priority actions. Think of it as the nervous system of your BabaBuilds ERP workspace. Instead of hunting through different modules to find out what needs attention, the Dashboard brings critical metrics and pending tasks directly to you.

<HelpImage src="/help/screenshots/help-dashboard-01-overview.png" alt="Dashboard overview with KPI cards" caption="Start your day here to scan business health quickly." />

## Key Metrics at a Glance

The top section of your dashboard features your vital KPI cards. These metrics provide a real-time snapshot of your business health:
- **Total Revenue:** Gross earnings across a defined period.
- **Outstanding Receivables:** Money owed to you by clients that has passed its due date.
- **Active Retainers:** The number of ongoing, recurring billing agreements.
- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR):** Predictable revenue derived from your active retainers.

## Action Queues and Alerts

Below the KPIs, the dashboard intelligently surfaces what needs your immediate attention.

<HelpImage src="/help/screenshots/help-dashboard-02-primary-action.png" alt="Dashboard with primary action areas" caption="Use quick actions and alerts to resolve high-priority blockers." />

### High-Priority Actions
You will see alerts for items such as:
- **Overdue Invoices:** Invoices that require follow-up.
- **Pending Approvals:** Drafts or expenses waiting for your review.
- **Expiring Retainers:** Contracts that need to be renewed soon to maintain cash flow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review Alerts">
    Scan the alerts section for any red indicators. These represent immediate financial or compliance risks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take Action">
    Click directly on an alert to jump to the relevant invoice or client record and resolve the issue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Trend Analysis and Deep Dives

To make informed decisions, you need context. The deeper sections of the dashboard provide visual trends and historical comparisons.

<HelpImage src="/help/screenshots/help-dashboard-03-detail.png" alt="Dashboard deeper detail section" caption="Detailed trend and activity context for decision-making." />

- **Revenue vs. Expenses Chart:** Quickly gauge profitability and identify months where expenses spiked unexpectedly.
- **Recent Activity Feed:** An audit log of the most recent actions taken by you or your team members in the workspace.

## Best Practices

**Do**
- Check the dashboard at the start and end of your workday to ensure no critical alerts are missed.
- Click through the KPI cards to view the underlying reports for more granular data.

**Don't**
- Ignore overdue invoice alerts; timely follow-ups are crucial for maintaining healthy cash flow.
- Rely solely on the dashboard for detailed tax preparation—always use the dedicated Tax and Reporting modules for end-of-year filings.

## FAQ

### How often do the dashboard metrics update?
Dashboard metrics update in real-time as transactions (like new invoices or recorded payments) occur across your workspace.

### Can I customize which KPI cards appear on my dashboard?
Currently, the dashboard displays a standardized set of KPIs optimized for general business health. Role-specific dashboard customization is planned for a future update.

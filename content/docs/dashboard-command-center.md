# Dashboard Command Center

The Dashboard answers a practical question: **what deserves attention now?** It brings together headline performance, receivables, upcoming work, trends, and recent activity so you can choose the next action without opening every module.

<HelpImage src="/product-images/light/dashboard.png" alt="BabaBuilds ERP dashboard with navigation, period controls, summaries, and activity modules" caption="Choose the reporting period first, then read from headline signals into the supporting detail." />

## Start with the period

Use the period selector near the page title before comparing totals. The selected financial year or accounting period controls the context for many dashboard figures. If a number looks unfamiliar, confirm the period before investigating the underlying records.

Use **Refresh** after another teammate records a payment, imports data, or completes an action in a separate tab.

## Read from signal to evidence

Work down the page in this order:

1. **Headline and KPI cards** — spot material changes, outstanding work, or unexpected values.
2. **Attention and agenda areas** — find overdue, blocked, or time-sensitive items.
3. **Receivables and cash summaries** — understand what is owed and how near-term cash is moving.
4. **Trends and recent activity** — check whether the signal is new, recurring, or explained by recent work.

Cards are summaries, not a second ledger. Open the related module when you need the records behind a figure.

## A useful daily routine

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm context">
    Check the active company and selected financial period. This is especially important when you manage more than one legal entity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear urgent work">
    Open overdue receivables, approvals, filing blockers, or other attention items and assign a concrete next step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review cash movement">
    Compare expected collections with recent expenses and the cash summary. Open Cash Flow for scenario planning rather than guessing from a single balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the audit trail">
    Scan recent activity for payments, invoice changes, imports, and team actions that explain the movement you see.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keep the dashboard trustworthy

- Record payments against their invoices.
- Approve or reject expense drafts instead of leaving them in review indefinitely.
- Reconcile imported bank activity and resolve duplicates.
- Keep invoice dates, due dates, and retainer status current.
- Use the correct company and period when importing or entering records.

Dashboard quality follows source-data quality. If a card is wrong, correct the underlying transaction or configuration; do not try to compensate with a separate manual entry.

## If a card is empty or still loading

1. Refresh the page once and confirm the network connection is stable.
2. Check whether the selected period contains data.
3. Confirm that your role can view the underlying module.
4. Open the related module to see whether it is empty, disabled, or waiting for setup.
5. If the page remains in a loading state, note the card name and time, then share that detail with support.

## What the dashboard is not for

Use the dedicated Tax, Reporting, Payroll, and Reconciliation workspaces for filing, exports, payroll approval, and ledger decisions. The Dashboard is an operating summary; it does not replace those review controls.

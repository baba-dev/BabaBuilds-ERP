# How to Choose and Compare Invoice Designs

You can preview every invoice design using your own latest invoice, then switch with one click.

<HelpImage src="/product-images/light/settings_invoicing.png" alt="Invoicing settings with the Compare all designs link, the invoice design picker and the accent colour field" caption="Settings → Invoicing has the design picker and a link to compare all designs." />

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Invoicing">
    Select **Settings** in the sidebar, then **Invoicing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Compare all designs">
    The page opens with your latest invoice in all eight designs. If you have no invoices yet, it shows a labelled sample.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a design full screen">
    Select any tile. Zoom in to read the detail, and use the arrows to move between designs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Use this design">
    The design applies to your workspace straight away. Only workspace managers can change it; everyone else can still preview and download.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- Previews never send an email or create a payment link.
- Changing the design does not change any invoice number or amount.
- Want to download a preview? Select **Download PDF** in the full-screen view.
